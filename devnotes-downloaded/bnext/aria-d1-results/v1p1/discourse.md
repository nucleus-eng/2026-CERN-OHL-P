## Claims

```

:::{claim} Unified Chelation Model for Polyphosphate's Role in Energy Systems
:label: claim-h1-unified-chelation-model-for-polyphosphat

Polyphosphate's primary role is as a potent magnesium (Mg2+) chelator. This single mechanism enables high-yield protein synthesis in PPK-driven systems at high Mg2+ levels and explains the viability of systems lacking dedicated energy regeneration enzymes by creating a stable ionic environment.
:::

:::{claim} Polyphosphate as a Scaffolding Agent for a Non-Enzymatic Translation Hub
:label: claim-h2-polyphosphate-as-a-scaffolding-agent-for

At high concentrations, Polyphosphate and Magnesium form a phase-separated scaffold that concentrates translational machinery. This physical organization, rather than enzymatic activity, sustains protein synthesis by preventing ATP precipitation and increasing local component concentrations.
:::

```
