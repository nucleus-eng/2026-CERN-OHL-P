# nucleus-rebuild-12/primer-build/
A build of pT5-CDS based on PCR amplification off of Ansa plasmids.

## nucleotide-perfect/
A HiFi build targeting precisely the insert sequence (from pT5 to STOP codon of each sequence). NOTE: due to the pT5 sequence, primers are sus. They may not work.

## with-flanking/
A HiFi build targeting the sequence immediatly up and downstream of the insert. Designed because the pT5 sequence requires sus primers.
